Features

Everything you need to stay on track

Task Flow combines task boards, smart filters, and analytics so you can focus on what matters.

Task management

Create, edit, and delete tasks with titles, descriptions, due dates, and priority levels.

Pending & completed

Separate views for active and finished work—mark complete or restore tasks anytime.

Custom categories

Organize tasks with color-coded categories like Work, Personal, and Health.

Priority & overdue

High, medium, and low priorities with visual overdue alerts on due dates.

Statistics dashboard

Charts and breakdowns for completion rate, priorities, and productivity insights.

Secure authentication

Email signup with verification, Google sign-in, and password reset via OTP email.

How it works

Up and running in three steps

No complicated setup—sign up, add your tasks, and start tracking progress immediately.

1

Create your account

Sign up with email or continue with Google. Verify your email and log in securely.

2

Add & organize tasks

Set priorities, due dates, and categories. Filter pending work from your dashboard.

3

Track your progress

Complete tasks, review stats, and stay ahead of deadlines with overdue highlights.
